Hangry Monsters Neutralized

COPPA & YouTube Kids Integration

Our videos at youtube.com/superfitmo are marked "Made for Kids". This automatically restricts tailored advertising, turns off comments on the video platform, and ensures child viewer data remains secure. No tracking follows your little heroes back home!

Social Media Protections

While we share dynamic rescue clips on Instagram & TikTok, these parent channels are designed strictly for adults. We encourage parents to handle the screen setup, using tools to launch the fitness dances for children so their personal screen profiles remain untracked.

Full Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: March 15, 2026

1

Information We Collect

Super Fitmo operates primarily as a public web-based hub for our video materials and branded apparel. We only collect user information in two circumstances: (a) voluntary information submitted via email/contact forms by parents (such as name, email address, and subject line), and (b) non-personally identifiable server traffic metrics compiled on a collective basis (e.g., total pages visited, country of origin, system configuration). We collect absolutely zero tracking cookies or identifiers targeting children under 13.

Parental Translation: We do not ask kids for names, emails, or phone numbers. If you (the parent) email us a question, we only keep your email to reply to your message!
2

Compliance with COPPA

Super Fitmo is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Our digital platforms are curated to be safely accessed by children under parental supervision. Our channel videos are flagged on platforms like YouTube Kids as "Made for Kids," which shuts down target tracking, cookies, and behavioral trackers. If we learn we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, we immediately wipe it from all records.

Parental Translation: We follow strict US government rules designed specifically to protect children under age 13 online. We work alongside YouTube to keep this zone locked down!
3

Third-Party Platform Links

This subpage integrates social embeds and redirects to our main media channels on third-party platforms (like Youtube and social networks). While we configure these to be as privacy-focused as possible, please note these sites operate independently under their own privacy policies. We encourage parents to review the settings on native apps like YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram to optimize safety controls.

Parental Translation: We play in the Youtube and Tiktok parks! Remember to check your parental control switches on those apps to maximize your household safety.
4

Parent Rights & Data Deletion

Parents maintain ultimate authority over their families' data records. You hold the legal right to request inspection of any records we hold (if any exist, such as support emails), request immediate modifications, or order permanent deletion of your info. Submit your direct request to our compliance officer at privacy@superfitmo.com and we will process the removal immediately.

Parental Translation: You are the boss! If you want us to completely erase an email thread or inquiry, just let us know and it's gone!
Contact Privacy Desk
