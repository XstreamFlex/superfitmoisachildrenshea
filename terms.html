Fighting Groggy Monsters Responsibly!

Before utilizing our songs, physical activities, and videos, please read these guidelines. Our main priority is giving kids a safe environment to learn, sing, jump, and grow.

01

By visiting, browsing, watching, or interacting with Super Fitmo’s physical products, music tracks, videos, printable packets, and services (including channels hosted at youtube.com/superfitmo, TikTok, Instagram, and this website), you represent that you are either of legal majority age (typically 18 years or older) or are viewing this with a parent or guardian who accepts these Terms of Service.

If you do not agree with any element of these terms, we kindly ask you and your children to refrain from accessing our videos, downloadables, and merchandise.

02

Parental Supervision Responsibility

Super Fitmo produces content strictly tailored for children's edutainment. However, parental supervision is absolutely required while kids execute physical routines, follow nutritional recommendations, or access external video streaming channels like YouTube and TikTok.

Important Guardian Reminder:

Always ensure children are playing in a room cleared of toys, hard furniture corners, and slick floors to prevent injury when they copy Super Fitmo's heroic jumps!

03

Fitness & Health Disclaimer

The songs, exercises, story-arcs, and nutritional messages shown in Super Fitmo content are purely for informational and edutainment entertainment.

Super Fitmo is a playful character, not a licensed pediatric health specialist, physical therapist, or medical practitioner. No educational media replaces tailored advice from your family pediatrician.

While we teach kids why sleeping, eating healthy veggies, and being active keep us from feeling like groggy monsters, you must consult your physician before modifying any strict fitness plans, diet routines, or therapy regimes.

Nutrition should be colorful and exciting, just like Super Fitmo's favorite banana!

05

Active Play Ground Rules

To rescue kids turned into groggy monsters, Super Fitmo videos encourage active jumping, stretches, and musical body movements. Users agree to adhere to the following play ground rules:

  • Check that children wear comfortable, slip-resistant shoes or play barefoot on a non-slip rug.
  • Stay fully hydrated! Have water cups within reach before initiating long video sessions.
  • If a child experiences exhaustion, muscle soreness, dizziness, or short breath, stop the play immediately and rest.
  • Never force a child to do stretches or exercises that cause them pain or discomfort.

Active play keeps the monsters away. Follow our rules for healthy and safe fun!
